Drafts

Everything under /drafts is safe scratch space: excluded from /query-index.json and /sitemap.xml, so nothing here shows up in search or product listings until you move/copy it out.

How to use this folder

1. Duplicate a starter

Copy /drafts/starter-page in DA (right-click → Duplicate) as the base for a new page.

2. Draft freely

Preview drafts the same way as any page — Sidekick → Preview. Nothing here publishes or gets indexed by accident.

3. Move when ready

When the page is ready, move/rename it out of /drafts to its real site path, then Preview + Publish from there.

Need the block reference?

Every insertable HYVR block has a labelled example under /block-library — open one, copy the table, paste it into your draft, then edit the content.
