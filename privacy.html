Privacy & Consent

Your data is yours. Here’s a plain-language look at what HYVR collects, what you get to control, and how to ask us to hand it back or delete it — no dark patterns, no fine-print traps.

This is a sample privacy overview for the HYVR demo. It is written to read realistically but it is not a legal document and is not legal advice.

What data we collect

When you browse HYVR we collect the basics needed to run the store: pages you view, the gear you look at, and technical details like your device type and approximate region. If you contact us or join the community, we keep what you share — your name, email, and messages — so we can help you. We only collect what we actually use.

Consent choices

You’re in charge of what happens beyond the essentials. Analytics and personalization are strictly opt-in: the scripts that power them are loaded only after you give consent, and not before. Decline and the site still works fully — you just won’t be measured or profiled. You can change your mind at any time and we’ll honour the new choice going forward.

Cookies

We use a small set of essential cookies to remember your session, cart and consent preferences — these are required for the site to function. Any analytics or personalization cookies are set only after you opt in, and are cleared if you withdraw consent. We don’t sell your data or hand it to advertisers for their own use.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Just send a request and we’ll verify it’s really you before we act, then respond within a reasonable time. Deleting your data may mean we can no longer provide certain features, and we’ll tell you if that’s the case.

Contact

Questions about your data, a consent choice, or an access or deletion request? Our privacy team is happy to help.
